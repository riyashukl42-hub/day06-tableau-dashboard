# Day 06 — Adidas US Sales Recovery Dashboard (Tableau Public)

## The story
How did Adidas US navigate a COVID-year sales collapse in 2020
and achieve a record recovery in 2021?

## Dataset
- Adidas US Sales 2020–2021
- 9,648 records · 6 retailers · 5 product categories · all US regions

## Dashboard narrative
1. Sales Recovery (2020 vs 2021) — the full story in one chart
2. Online Rose as In-Store Fell — how the channel pivot saved revenue
3. West Gear Led the Recovery — top retailer performance
4. Men's Street Footwear Dominated — product category winners
5. West Coast Revenue Map — geographic strength

## Key findings
- 2021 total sales significantly exceeded 2020
- Online sales surged in 2020 and stayed elevated in 2021
- Men's Street Footwear was the highest revenue AND profit category
- West Gear emerged as the top retailer in 2021

## Recommendation
Maintain online channel investment. Prioritise Men's Street Footwear
in West region for continued growth.
